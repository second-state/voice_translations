TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Ukrainian):
- Use natural modern spoken Ukrainian; address strangers and elders with ви.
- Keep foreign names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Ukrainian):
- The ти/ви choice is the register: ви in business and formal calls, ти among friends; consistent per speaker.
- Formal occasions take established phrasing (шановні колеги, дякуємо вам); casual speech its own particles (ну, от, давай).
- Use modern standard Ukrainian vocabulary throughout.
