CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Hong Kong Cantonese):
- Every register of Hong Kong Cantonese is still spoken Cantonese: even the most formal toast or announcement uses 係/唔/嘅 grammar, never 書面語. Formality comes from vocabulary and address (各位嘉賓, 多謝各位), not from switching to written Chinese.
- Business Cantonese in Hong Kong code-mixes English constantly (今日個meeting, send返個file畀我, con call); keep the English words a HK professional would use.
- Casual calls get the full particle set (啦, 喎, 囉, 㗎, 咋) and colloquial vocabulary; friends do not speak to each other in neutral textbook Cantonese.
