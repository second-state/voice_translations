CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Hindi):
- आप in business and formal calls, तुम among friends (तू only for the closest, clearly playful registers); keep the choice per speaker.
- Hinglish is the working register of Indian professional life: business and tech calls keep English chunks (meeting, deadline, approve kar do) inside Hindi sentences.
- Casual Hindi is particle- and interjection-rich (यार, अरे, बस) - use them there, never in formal renderings.
