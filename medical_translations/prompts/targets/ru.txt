TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Russian):
- Use natural spoken Russian; address strangers and elders with вы.
- Keep foreign names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Russian):
- Use everyday clinical Russian: анализ крови, рентген, повторный приём, аллергия на лекарство, давление; match the speaker's register between lay and technical.
- Keep drug names as spoken (original or Russian rendering) and test acronyms (КТ, МРТ, ЭКГ) in their usual Russian form; doses stay as digits with their unit (5 мг, два раза в день).
