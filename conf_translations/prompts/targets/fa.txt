TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Persian):
- Use natural spoken Persian with the polite شما toward strangers and elders.
- Keep foreign names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Persian):
- شما in business and formal calls, تو among friends; formal Persian carries taarof courtesy formulas (خواهش می‌کنم, لطف دارید) - use them where the occasion calls, without adding content.
- Professional Persian keeps common English loans (میتینگ, ددلاین) where Iranian professionals use them.
- Casual Persian is warm and particle-rich (دیگه, آخه, بابا) - keep the temperature of the original.
