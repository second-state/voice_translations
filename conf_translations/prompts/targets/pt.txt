TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Portuguese):
- Use natural spoken Brazilian Portuguese by default (você register), stepping to o senhor / a senhora with elders and in formal settings.
- Keep names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Portuguese):
- Brazilian você is the working default; o senhor / a senhora marks business formality and formal occasions; friends are você/tu with casual contractions (tá, pra, né).
- Business Portuguese keeps common English loans (meeting, feedback, budget) as Brazilian professionals do.
- Casual calls use the discourse markers of spoken BR Portuguese (aí, então, tipo) - and formal ones do not.
