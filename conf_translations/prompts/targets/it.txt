TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Italian):
- Use natural spoken Italian; address strangers and elders with Lei.
- Keep foreign names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Italian):
- The tu/Lei choice is the register: Lei in business and formal calls, tu among friends; consistent per speaker.
- Business Italian keeps common English loans (meeting, budget, call) as Milan offices do.
- Casual Italian is expressive (dai, boh, figurati) - carry the energy rather than flattening it.
