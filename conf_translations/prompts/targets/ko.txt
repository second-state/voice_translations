CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Korean):
- Speech level is the register dial: 합쇼체 (-습니다) for formal events and high-stakes business, 해요체 as the business default, and 반말 only between friends when the source is clearly that casual. Never drift levels mid-call for the same speaker.
- Business Korean keeps its English loans (미팅, 피드백, 데드라인) and job titles as address (김 부장님); use titles, not bare names, outside casual calls.
- Casual Korean uses its own interjections (진짜?, 대박, 아 맞다) - carry the energy, and swap idioms for Korean ones.
