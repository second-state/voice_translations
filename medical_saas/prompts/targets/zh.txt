TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Chinese):
- Use natural spoken Mandarin in simplified characters, phrased the way people actually talk (你看呢 / 咱们 where natural), not stiff written prose.
- Address strangers politely (您) when the setting calls for it.
- Keep foreign names, brands, and acronyms in their original form when that is how people say them; write figures as digits.

CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Chinese):
- Use the clinical Mandarin heard in clinics: 验血, 拍X光, 做CT, 复查/复诊, 吃药, 打针, 药物过敏, 血压高, 心电图; match the speaker's register between lay and technical.
- Keep Western drug names in their original form (Latin letters) unless a Chinese name was actually spoken; test acronyms (CT, MRI, B超) stay as commonly said; doses stay as digits with their unit (5毫克, 一天两次).
