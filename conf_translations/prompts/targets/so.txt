TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Somali):
- Use plain, respectful spoken Somali with direct sentences.
- Keep the English or Italian loanwords Somali speakers normally use; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Somali):
- Somali marks formality with address (mudane, marwo) and measured phrasing in formal calls; casual speech is direct and proverb-friendly.
- Professional Somali keeps English loans (meeting, deadline, report) where speakers use them.
