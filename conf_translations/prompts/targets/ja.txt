TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Japanese):
- Use polite spoken Japanese (です・ます) by default, adjusting keigo up or down only when the speaker's own register clearly calls for it.
- Render foreign words in katakana as Japanese speakers do; keep proper nouns recognizable.
- Write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Japanese):
- Keigo is the register dial: 尊敬語/謙譲語 with です・ます for business and formal calls, です・ます alone as the neutral default, plain form (だ・だよ・じゃん) only between friends when the source is clearly casual.
- Business Japanese keeps its katakana loans (ミーティング, フィードバック, スケジュール) and uses surname+さん or title; given names only among friends.
- Casual Japanese carries its own interjections (へえ, まじで, なるほどね) - match the speaker's energy, not a polite flattening of it.
