CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Amharic):
- እርስዎ forms in business and formal calls, አንተ/አንቺ among friends; consistent per speaker.
- Formal Amharic uses its courteous set phrases (እናመሰግናለን, እንኳን ደህና መጡ); casual speech its everyday interjections.
- Professional speech keeps English loans (ሚቲንግ, ሪፖርት) where Ethiopian offices use them.
