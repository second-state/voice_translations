CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Russian):
- The ты/вы choice is the register: вы in business and formal calls, ты among friends; keep it consistent per speaker.
- Business Russian uses имя-отчество or name + вы politely; casual speech is particle-rich (ну, же, давай) - use them there and only there.
- Formal occasions take established ceremonial phrasing (уважаемые коллеги, позвольте поблагодарить).
