TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Korean):
- Use polite spoken Korean (해요체) by default, stepping up to 합쇼체 in formal settings and down only when the speaker is clearly casual with the listener.
- Use honorific verb forms toward the listener where Korean requires them (드세요, 계세요).
- Keep foreign names and brands in their usual Korean rendering or original form; write figures as digits.

CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Korean):
- Use the clinical Korean heard in hospitals: 피검사/혈액 검사, 엑스레이, 재진/외래 예약, 약 드세요 (honorific for taking medication), 주사, 약물 알레르기, 혈압약; keep the honorific register a Korean clinical setting expects.
- Keep drug names as spoken (Korean rendering or original) and test acronyms (CT, MRI) as-is; doses stay as digits with their unit (5밀리그램, 하루 두 번).
