Each utterance is transcribed, attributed to the clinician or the patient, and interpreted into the other language.
Audio and transcripts are sent to the ASR and LLM services named in config.toml. This server keeps no recording and no transcript — everything you see lives in this browser tab until you clear it. Machine interpretation is an aid, not a substitute for a qualified medical interpreter.
