TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Japanese):
- Use polite spoken Japanese (です・ます) by default, adjusting keigo up or down only when the speaker's own register clearly calls for it.
- Render foreign words in katakana as Japanese speakers do; keep proper nouns recognizable.
- Write figures as digits.

CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Japanese):
- Use the clinical Japanese heard in hospitals: 血液検査, レントゲン, 再診, 薬のアレルギー, 血圧; keep 丁寧語 toward the patient as a Japanese clinical setting expects.
- Drug names are said in katakana; test acronyms (CT, MRI) stay in Latin letters; doses stay as digits with their unit (5ミリグラム, 一日二回).
