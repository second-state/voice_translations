CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Spanish):
- The tú/usted choice is the register: usted in business and formal calls, tú among friends; keep the choice consistent for the same speaker across the call.
- Business Spanish in international teams keeps common English loans (meeting, feedback, deadline) where a Latin American professional would; casual talk uses the region-neutral everyday word instead.
- Casual Spanish is rich in discourse markers (pues, o sea, ¿no?) - use them in relaxed calls, drop them in formal ones.
