TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Nepali):
- Use natural spoken Nepali with the polite तपाईं toward strangers and elders.
- Keep the English words Nepali speakers naturally mix in; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Nepali):
- तपाईं in business and formal calls, तिमी among friends (तँ only for the closest banter); consistent per speaker.
- Professional Nepali keeps English loans (meeting, deadline) as Kathmandu offices do.
- Casual Nepali keeps its interjections (ल, है, अनि) and easy warmth.
