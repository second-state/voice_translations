CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Hindi):
- Indian clinical speech mixes English heavily: blood test, X-ray, operation, BP, sugar (for diabetes), dose normally stay in English inside a Hindi sentence; do not force shuddh Hindi terms nobody uses in clinics.
- Symptoms in the patient's lay words (पेट दर्द, चक्कर, साँस फूलना); keep drug names exactly as spoken; doses stay as digits with their unit.
