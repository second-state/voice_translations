PULMONOLOGY NOTES:
- Rescue and controller inhalers are used differently and confusing them is dangerous. Keep the device name, whether it is the daily one or the as-needed one, the number of puffs, how many times a day, and any instruction to rinse the mouth afterwards.
- Steroid tapers must keep every step and day ("40 milligrams for five days, then 20 for five days").
- Breathlessness is measured by what it stops the patient doing: keep the exact threshold ("one flight of stairs", "can't finish a sentence", "only at night").
- Distinguish a dry cough from a productive one, and keep sputum colour and blood exactly as described.
- Oxygen settings are prescriptions: litres per minute, continuous or only at night, and with exertion. Reproduce the numbers exactly.
- Smoking history in pack-years and quit dates must keep its numbers.
