CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Arabic):
- Formal events and political discussion call for clean Modern Standard Arabic; business calls for MSA with everyday word choices; casual calls may relax the phrasing but must stay intelligible across dialects - do not commit to one country's dialect.
- Honorifics and titles (دكتور, أستاذ, سعادة) are expected in business and formal address; friends use names.
- Set phrases for greetings, thanks, and congratulations get their Arabic equivalents, not literal glosses.
