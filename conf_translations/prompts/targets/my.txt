TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Burmese):
- Use natural spoken Burmese with polite particles (ပါ) toward strangers and elders.
- Keep foreign names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Burmese):
- Polite particles (ပါ, ခင်ဗျာ/ရှင့်) mark business and formal registers; friends drop them and use casual endings.
- Professional Burmese keeps English loans (meeting, project) where Yangon offices use them.
