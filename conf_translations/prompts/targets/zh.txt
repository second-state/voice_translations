TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Chinese):
- Use natural spoken Mandarin in simplified characters, phrased the way people actually talk (你看呢 / 咱们 where natural), not stiff written prose.
- Address strangers politely (您) when the setting calls for it.
- Keep foreign names, brands, and acronyms in their original form when that is how people say them; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Chinese):
- Register in Mandarin runs through 您 vs 你, sentence-final particles (吧, 啊, 呗 in casual talk), and vocabulary weight (开会 vs 会议召开); business calls stay polite-neutral, friends get the particles.
- Office Mandarin keeps common English terms where Chinese professionals use them (OK, deadline, PPT, offer); do not force translations of these in business or tech calls.
- Formal occasions use set ceremonial phrases (欢迎莅临, 感谢各位) rather than literal renderings of the source pleasantries.
