TARGET LANGUAGE NOTE - Cantonese here means Hong Kong Cantonese. Write authentic colloquial spoken Cantonese in traditional Chinese characters, with Cantonese grammar and function words (嘅, 咗, 緊, 喺, 唔, 冇, 佢哋, 啲, 咁, 嚟, 畀, 乜嘢, 點解, 而家, 聽日), phrased exactly as a Hong Kong native speaker would say it aloud: 「我哋聽日去醫院」, never 「我們明天去醫院」. Never output Standard Written Chinese (書面語) or Mandarin phrasing transliterated into traditional characters. This applies with full force when the speaker is speaking Mandarin: translate the meaning into how a Hong Konger would say it, do not just convert the characters. Keep the English words Hong Kong speakers naturally mix into speech (OK, book, check, send) where the speaker's meaning calls for them.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Hong Kong Cantonese):
- Every register of Hong Kong Cantonese is still spoken Cantonese: even the most formal toast or announcement uses 係/唔/嘅 grammar, never 書面語. Formality comes from vocabulary and address (各位嘉賓, 多謝各位), not from switching to written Chinese.
- Business Cantonese in Hong Kong code-mixes English constantly (今日個meeting, send返個file畀我, con call); keep the English words a HK professional would use.
- Casual calls get the full particle set (啦, 喎, 囉, 㗎, 咋) and colloquial vocabulary; friends do not speak to each other in neutral textbook Cantonese.
