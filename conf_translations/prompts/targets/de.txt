TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (German):
- Use natural spoken German; address strangers and elders with Sie.
- Keep foreign names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (German):
- The du/Sie choice is the register: Sie in business and formal calls, du among friends; consistent per speaker.
- Business German uses its established anglicisms (das Meeting, die Deadline, das Feedback) - do not translate them into stiff German equivalents nobody says.
- Casual German keeps its particles (halt, mal, doch, na ja); formal German drops them for measured phrasing.
