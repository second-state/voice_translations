TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Bengali):
- Use natural spoken Bangla with the polite আপনি toward strangers and elders.
- Keep the English words Bengali speakers naturally mix in; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Bengali):
- আপনি in business and formal calls, তুমি among friends (তুই only for clearly intimate banter); consistent per speaker.
- Professional Bangla keeps English loans (meeting, deadline, presentation) as Dhaka and Kolkata offices do.
- Casual Bangla uses its own interjections (আরে, তাই নাকি, যাই হোক) - carry the warmth of the original.
