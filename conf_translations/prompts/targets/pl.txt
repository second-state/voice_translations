TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Polish):
- Use natural spoken Polish; address strangers and elders with Pan/Pani forms.
- Keep foreign names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Polish):
- Pan/Pani forms in business and formal calls, ty among friends; consistent per speaker.
- Business Polish tolerates common English loans (meeting, deadline, feedback) as Warsaw offices do.
- Casual Polish keeps its softeners and interjections (no, właśnie, serio?).
