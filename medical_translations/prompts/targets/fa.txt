CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Persian):
- Use everyday clinical Persian: آزمایش خون, عکس, ویزیت پیگیری, حساسیت دارویی, فشار خون; match the speaker's register between lay and technical.
- Keep drug names in their original form as written on prescriptions; test acronyms (سی‌تی, ام‌آرآی) in their usual Persian rendering; doses stay as digits with their unit.
