PHYSICAL THERAPY NOTES:
- An exercise prescription is a set of numbers: how many repetitions, how many sets, how many times a day, how many days a week, how long to hold, how much resistance. Every one of them must survive.
- Movement directions and body parts must be exact and side-specific. Bending, straightening, lifting to the side, rotating inward, and the specific joint are not interchangeable.
- Keep pain rules exactly as stated: what level of discomfort is acceptable, what means stop, and what means call the clinic. "Some soreness is fine, sharp pain means stop" must not collapse into "stop if it hurts".
- Preserve ice-versus-heat instructions with their durations.
- Restrictions carry timeframes ("no lifting over 10 pounds for four weeks"); keep the limit, the unit, and the duration.
