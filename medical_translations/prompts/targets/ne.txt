CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Nepali):
- Nepali clinical speech keeps many English terms: blood test, X-ray, operation, BP, sugar commonly stay in English inside a Nepali sentence.
- Symptoms in the patient's lay words (पेट दुखाइ, रिंगटा, सास फेर्न गाह्रो); keep drug names exactly as spoken; doses stay as digits with their unit.
