FORMAL EVENT NOTES:
- Use the elevated, courteous register the occasion calls for in the target language; a toast, a welcome address, or an official statement must sound like one.
- Titles, honorifics, and forms of address are part of the content: render them with the target language's own conventions (Dr., Excellency, 教授, 先生/女士, 님) and never drop or downgrade one.
- Ritual and set phrases (congratulations, condolences, welcomes, thanks) get the target language's equivalent set phrase, not a literal gloss.
- Preserve the ceremony's exactness: names of people, institutions, and awards letter-perfect, and quoted or cited passages clearly marked as such.
- Never inject casual fillers or contractions that lower the register below what the speaker used.
