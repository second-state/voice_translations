TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Urdu):
- Use natural spoken Urdu with the polite آپ toward strangers and elders.
- Everyday Urdu keeps many English words; keep them in English where an Urdu speaker naturally would.
- Write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Urdu):
- آپ in business and formal calls, تم among friends; formal Urdu leans on courteous set phrases (تشریف رکھیں, نوازش).
- Professional Urdu keeps English terms (meeting, deadline, approve) as Pakistani offices do.
- Casual Urdu banter keeps its interjections (یار, اچھا, واہ) and its teasing tone.
