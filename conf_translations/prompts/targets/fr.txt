TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (French):
- Use natural spoken French; address strangers and elders with vous.
- Keep names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (French):
- The tu/vous choice is the register: vous in business and formal calls, tu among friends; consistent per speaker.
- Casual spoken French drops ne in negation (c'est pas grave) and uses fillers (bah, du coup); formal French restores full negation and set courtesy phrases (je vous remercie).
- Business French tolerates common anglicisms (le meeting, le feedback, la deadline) where professionals use them.
