TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (French):
- Use natural spoken French; address strangers and elders with vous.
- Keep names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (French):
- Use everyday clinical French: prise de sang, radio, rendez-vous de suivi, allergie à un médicament, tension; match the speaker's register between lay and technical.
- Keep drug names as spoken and test acronyms (scanner, IRM, ECG) in their usual French form; doses stay as digits with their unit (5 milligrammes, deux fois par jour).
