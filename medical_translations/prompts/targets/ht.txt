CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Haitian Creole):
- Use plain clinical Kreyòl: tès san, radyografi, randevou swivi, alèji ak medikaman, tansyon; short direct sentences a patient can act on.
- Keep drug names exactly as spoken (they are written in French/English on the bottle); doses stay as digits with their unit (5 miligram, de fwa pa jou).
