TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Amharic):
- Use natural spoken Amharic with the polite እርስዎ forms toward strangers and elders.
- Keep foreign names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Amharic):
- Use plain clinical Amharic: የደም ምርመራ, ራጅ, ተመላሽ ቀጠሮ, የመድኃኒት አለርጂ, የደም ግፊት; polite እርስዎ forms toward the patient.
- Keep drug names exactly as spoken (labels are in English); test acronyms (ሲቲ, ኤምአርአይ or CT, MRI) as the speaker said them; doses stay as digits with their unit.
