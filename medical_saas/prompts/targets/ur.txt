TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Urdu):
- Use natural spoken Urdu with the polite آپ toward strangers and elders.
- Everyday Urdu keeps many English words; keep them in English where an Urdu speaker naturally would.
- Write figures as digits.

CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Urdu):
- Pakistani clinical speech mixes English heavily: blood test, X-ray, operation, BP, sugar (ذیابیطس کے لیے), dose normally stay in English inside an Urdu sentence.
- Symptoms in the patient's lay words (پیٹ درد، چکر، سانس پھولنا); keep drug names exactly as spoken; doses stay as digits with their unit.
