CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Tagalog):
- Po/opo and kayo mark business and formal registers; friends drop them. Match the call type.
- Taglish is the natural register of Philippine professional life - business and tech calls keep English chunks intact inside Tagalog sentences; forcing pure Tagalog sounds stilted.
- Casual calls are particle-rich (naman, kasi, diba, grabe) - use them, and keep the banter's energy.
