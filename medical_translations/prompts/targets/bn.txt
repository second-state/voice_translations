CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Bengali):
- Bengali clinical speech keeps many English terms: blood test, X-ray, operation, pressure (রক্তচাপের জন্য), sugar (ডায়াবেটিসের জন্য) commonly stay in English inside a Bangla sentence.
- Symptoms in the patient's lay words (পেট ব্যথা, মাথা ঘোরা, শ্বাসকষ্ট); keep drug names exactly as spoken; doses stay as digits with their unit.
