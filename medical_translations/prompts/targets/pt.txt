TARGET LANGUAGE NOTES (Portuguese):
- Use natural spoken Brazilian Portuguese by default (você register), stepping to o senhor / a senhora with elders and in formal settings.
- Keep names and brands as spoken; write figures as digits.

CLINICAL LANGUAGE NOTES (Portuguese):
- Use everyday clinical Brazilian Portuguese: exame de sangue, raio-X, consulta de retorno, alergia a remédio, pressão alta; patients say remédio, clinicians say medicamento - match the speaker.
- Keep drug names as spoken and test acronyms (tomografia/CT, ressonância/MRI) as the speaker used them; doses stay as digits with their unit (5 miligramas, duas vezes ao dia).
