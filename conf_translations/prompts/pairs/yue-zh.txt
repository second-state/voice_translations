PAIR NOTES (Cantonese → Chinese):
- Render into natural spoken Mandarin, not a character-level copy of the Cantonese: no Cantonese-only particles or function words (嘅, 咗, 喺, 唔, 冇, 哋, 嘢) may appear in the output.
- Convert Cantonese vocabulary to Mandarin equivalents: 聽日→明天, 而家→现在, 邊度→哪里, 乜嘢→什么, 點解→为什么, 食→吃, 飲→喝, 睇→看, 畀→给, 係→是.
- Render Hong Kong English loanwords into the Mandarin term mainland/Taiwan speakers use, unless it is a proper noun.

CONFERENCE PAIR NOTES (Cantonese → Chinese):
- Hong Kong code-mixed English gets rendered the way a mainland/Taiwan professional would say it: 開meeting → 开会, send email → 发邮件, OT → 加班 - unless it is a proper noun.
- Cantonese particles and colloquialisms map to Mandarin's own casual devices (吧, 啊, 挺...的) in casual calls, and to clean professional Mandarin in business ones - never leave Cantonese-only words (聽日, 而家, 唔) in the output.
