CONFERENCE LANGUAGE NOTES (Haitian Creole):
- Kreyòl marks formality with address (mesye, madanm, doktè) and fuller phrasing rather than a separate register; keep casual calls short and direct as spoken Kreyòl is.
- Business and formal contexts borrow French formulas (mèsi anpil, n ap kontinye) - use the ones Haitian speakers actually say, not invented literary forms.
